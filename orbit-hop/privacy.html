Orbit Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Orbit Hop (the "Game"), a mobile game published by IASL Games ("we", "us"). We keep it short and in plain language.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account.
  • Your scores and settings stay on your phone.
  • The Game is free because it shows ads. Our ad partners collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, we ask your permission before personalised ads are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Information stored only on your device

The Game saves a few things on your phone so it works the way you left it: your best score, sound and vibration settings, and your ad-privacy choice. This information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Information collected by our advertising partners

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay (by Unity Technologies, which includes ironSource) to show ads, including through the Unity Ads network. To choose, deliver, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (country or city level);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language, screen size and network type;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with (for example, whether an ad was viewed or tapped).

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you personally. We only see combined reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

These partners process the information under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity – Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • ironSource – ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy

3. Your choices

  • Personalised ads (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland): the first time you open the Game you can choose Accept or Decline. If you decline, you still get the full game; ads are simply less relevant. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button on the title screen.
  • Everywhere: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. The "Continue" ad only plays when you tap it.
  • Stopping all collection: uninstall the Game.

4. Legal basis (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Contextual (non-personalised) advertising and fraud prevention are based on our and our partners' legitimate interest in funding a free game and keeping ads safe.

5. US state privacy rights

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws may have the right to know about, delete, or opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, the most effective way to exercise these rights is through your device's ad settings (section 3) and the partner links in section 2. You can also contact us and we will help.

6. Children

Orbit Hop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will work with our partners to delete it.

7. Data retention, security and transfers

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game. Our ad partners keep the information they collect for the periods described in their policies and may process it in countries other than yours, using legally recognised safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@iaslgames.com
